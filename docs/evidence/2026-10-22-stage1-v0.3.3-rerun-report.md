Average RPM is an event-spacing calculation under the one-detection-per-revolution assumption, not a calibrated finger-test rotation measurement.

Raw transitions include both polarities and chatter. Zero rejected qualified detections does not mean no chatter: stable-level qualification suppresses transitions before PulseCounter rejection accounting. Raw transitions minus accepted detections is not a valid count of rejected cycles.

## Conclusion

Updated firmware builds, uploads and responds to input, with stable counters after activity. Known-count accuracy and one flash per physical cycle are not independently established. Controlled mark/revolution counts, measured sensing conditions, pulse widths and RPM range remain pending. No full Stage 1 acceptance or change to outstanding Stage 0B gates.
