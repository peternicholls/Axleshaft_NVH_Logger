## Interpretation and limits

One eligible slot was missed (approximately 0.0167%); no queue overflow was reported. This is a failed continuity acceptance test, despite adequate observed queue headroom. The 44.564 ms maximum is an observation, not a latency bound. This card differs in capacity from the original tiny Stage 0A SDSC; its card type is not established by capacity alone.

The combined firmware error does not independently identify read-back outcome. No read-back pass or corruption diagnosis is inferred. The counters do not locate the missed slot: it could be an acquisition delay or an unserviced slot counted at stop. Queue headroom does not establish producer scheduling continuity.
